System Architecture & 1-Year Retention Policy

Designed in pure C & modern web frontend

1. Linked List Inventory

The active inventory is managed in a dynamic singly linked list (ItemNode*). Items can be inserted in $O(1)$ time, sorted by ID, traversed, searched, or removed upon claim without fixed array memory limits.

2. First-In, First-Out (FIFO) Claim Queue

Claim requests are queued sequentially (ClaimQueue). This prevents favoritism or race conditions, ensuring administrators review ownership proof on a strict chronological basis.

3. Statutory 1-Year Retention Database (claim_history.txt)

Why a 1-Year Archive? College personal property (laptops, phones, watches, bags) carries legal liability. If an item is fraudulently claimed or disputed, administrators must maintain an immutable audit trail.

  • Audit Trail: Captures claimant Full Name, University USN, Contact Phone, and verified College Email.
  • 365-Day Retention: ISO timestamps are stamped on every transaction for scheduled annual purging and accreditation audit checks.
